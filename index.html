Track gas sensor output, air quality level, and pollutant trend in one small web app.

This dashboard reads JSON sent by the ESP32, estimates an air-quality score from the MQ-135 analog value, and shows a simple pollutant level label suitable for school or hobby projects.

ESP32 endpoint

Your ESP32 serves live sensor data from /data. Open this page and replace the URL in API_URL with your ESP32 IP address.

Example JSON:
{
  "raw": 412,
  "voltage": 1.33,
  "airQuality": "Moderate",
  "co2ppm": 624,
  "nh3Level": "Low",
  "vocLevel": "Medium"
}
